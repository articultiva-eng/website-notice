articultivate

/ɑːrˈtɪk.jəl.tɪ.veɪt/  ·  ar-TIC-yul-tih-vayt
verb · English neologism

A newly coined term presented here with its intended definition, pronunciation, and examples of use. This page also documents the creator’s claim of authorship.

1. Meaning

articultivate (verb): To cultivate art

To promote appreciation and presence of art in everyday life.

2. Examples of usage

  • “Through exhibitions and workshops, the organization hopes to articultivate its community.”
  • “Her goal as an educator is to articultivate young people by encouraging them to create and appreciate art.”
  • “By bringing handcrafted ceramics into everyday spaces, Articultiva seeks to articultivate.”

3. Origin and related name

The word articultivate was coined by Nemanja Dzokic in 2025 by combining arti-, referring to art, with cultivate, to express the idea of cultivating art. The term also inspired the name of Articultiva, the handcrafted decorative ceramics business he founded, whose mission reflects this same concept.

The business name Articultiva is derived from articultivate and reflects the company’s mission: to cultivate art, foster good taste, and enrich people’s lives by transforming their living spaces with an exclusive collection of handcrafted decorative products.
